# ShopEase Refund, Return and Replacement Policy

## Return Window

- Physical products can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags attached.
- Digital products (ShopEase Learn) can be refunded within 7 days of purchase, only if the content has not been downloaded more than once.
- SaaS subscriptions (Storefront, Insights) can be refunded within 14 days of the first payment. Renewals are not refundable but can be cancelled at any time.
- Marketing services (ShopEase Growth) are not refundable once a monthly campaign has started.

## How to Return a Physical Product

1. Go to My Orders and select the order.
2. Click Return Item and choose a reason.
3. Print the prepaid return label or choose a free doorstep pickup.
4. Pack the item securely and hand it to the courier.
5. After our warehouse receives and checks the item, the refund is started within 2 business days.

## Refund Timelines

- Refund goes back to the original payment method.
- Credit or debit card: 5 to 7 business days after the refund is started.
- UPI and net banking: 3 to 5 business days.
- ShopEase wallet: instant.
- Shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error or a damaged item.

## Damaged or Defective Items

- Report a damaged or defective item within 7 days of delivery.
- Send 2 clear photos of the item and the packaging, through My Orders, Report a Problem.
- We review the report within 24 hours.
- Replacement: the same item is shipped free of charge within 3 to 5 business days of approval. A free pickup of the damaged item is arranged.
- If the item is out of stock, you can choose a full refund instead.

## Non-Returnable Items

- Items marked Final Sale.
- Innerwear and personal care items once opened.
- Downloaded licence keys.
