# ShopEase Shipping Policy

Applies to physical products from ShopEase Marketplace.

## Delivery Timelines

- Standard shipping: 5 to 7 business days. Free on orders above Rs. 999, otherwise Rs. 79.
- Express shipping: 2 to 3 business days. Costs Rs. 199.
- Same-day delivery: available in selected metro cities for orders placed before 11:00 AM. Costs Rs. 249.
- Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of payment confirmation. Weekends and public holidays are not counted as business days.

## Tracking Your Order

- A tracking link is sent by email and SMS when the order is dispatched.
- You can also open My Orders, select the order and click Track Package.
- Tracking updates can take up to 12 hours to appear after dispatch.

## Delayed Orders

- An order is considered delayed when it has not arrived 2 business days after the estimated delivery date.
- If your order is late but within 2 business days of the estimated date, please wait. Carrier delays during peak periods are common.
- For a delayed order, contact support (email support@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) with your order ID. We will contact the courier and give you an update within 24 hours.
- Delay compensation: for orders delayed by more than 5 business days, we credit a Rs. 100 coupon to your account.
- If an order has not arrived 15 business days after dispatch, it is treated as lost. We then ship a replacement at no cost or give a full refund, whichever you choose.

## Delivery Problems

- Wrong address: the address can be changed only before dispatch, from My Orders, Edit Address.
- Failed delivery: the courier makes 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel returns to our warehouse and the customer is contacted.
- Order shows delivered but not received: check with neighbours and building security, then contact support (email support@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) within 48 hours.
