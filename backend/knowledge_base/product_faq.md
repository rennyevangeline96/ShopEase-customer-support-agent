# ShopEase Product FAQ

## Physical Products

Q: How long is the warranty on appliances such as blenders and kettles?
A: All home appliances have a 12-month manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Warranty covers manufacturing defects, not misuse or drops.

Q: The item I received is broken or not working. What should I do?
A: Report it within 7 days of delivery through My Orders, Report a Problem, with 2 photos. We offer a free replacement or a full refund. See the Refund Policy for details.

Q: Can I exchange shoes or clothes for a different size?
A: Yes. Choose Exchange instead of Return in My Orders within 30 days. The new size ships once we receive the first item, or immediately if you pay the difference upfront.

## ShopEase Storefront (SaaS)

Q: What plans are available?
A: Basic at Rs. 799 per month, Pro at Rs. 1,999 per month and Business at Rs. 4,999 per month. Yearly billing gives 2 months free.

Q: How do I connect my own domain?
A: Go to Settings, Domains, click Connect Domain and add the CNAME record shown to your domain provider. It can take up to 24 hours to become active.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Go to Settings, Billing, Cancel Plan. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

## ShopEase Insights (SaaS)

Q: Why is my dashboard not updating?
A: Data refreshes every 15 minutes. If it is older than 1 hour, open Settings, Data Sources and click Reconnect. Check that your API key has not expired. API keys expire after 90 days.

Q: How do I export a report?
A: Open the report, click Export and choose CSV or PDF. Reports above 100,000 rows are emailed to you.

## ShopEase Learn (Digital Products)

Q: My download link does not work.
A: Download links expire after 72 hours. Open My Library and click Download again to get a fresh link. You have lifetime access.

Q: A course video will not play.
A: Try another browser, disable ad blockers and check your internet speed (minimum 3 Mbps). If it still fails, contact support (email support@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) with a screenshot.

## ShopEase Growth (Marketing Service)

Q: When do I get my monthly report?
A: Reports are shared by email on the 5th of every month.

Q: Can I change my package?
A: Yes. Packages can be upgraded at any time and downgraded at the start of the next month. Starter is Rs. 15,000 per month, Pro is Rs. 35,000 per month and Premium is Rs. 70,000 per month.
