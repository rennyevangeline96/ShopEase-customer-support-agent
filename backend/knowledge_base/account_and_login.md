# ShopEase Account and Login Help

One ShopEase account works for Marketplace, Storefront, Insights and Learn.

## Reset a Forgotten Password

1. Go to the login page and click Forgot Password.
2. Enter the email address used for your account.
3. Open the reset email. It arrives within 5 minutes. Check the spam folder if you do not see it.
4. Click the reset link. The link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once.
5. Create a new password with at least 10 characters, including one number and one symbol.
6. Sign in with the new password.

If the reset email does not arrive, wait 10 minutes and try again. Make sure you typed the same email address you registered with.

## Locked Account

- After 5 wrong password attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes.
- Wait 30 minutes, or reset your password to unlock it immediately.
- If you still cannot sign in after a reset, contact support (email support@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) from a different device.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- 2FA codes are sent by SMS or an authenticator app and expire after 60 seconds.
- If you do not receive the SMS code, check your phone signal and request a new code after 60 seconds.
- If you lost the phone with your authenticator, use one of your 8 backup codes shown when you enabled 2FA.
- If you have no backup codes, support can reset 2FA after verifying your identity.

## Change Email or Phone Number

- Go to Account Settings, Profile and click Edit.
- A verification code is sent to the new email or phone before the change is saved.

## Delete Account

- Go to Account Settings, Privacy and click Delete Account. Deletion is completed within 14 days and cannot be undone.
