# ShopEase Contact and Support

## How to Contact ShopEase Support

- Email: support@shopease.example. We reply within 24 hours.
- Phone: 1800-123-4567 (toll free). Open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST.
- In the app or website: My Orders, select the order and click Help to open a support request linked to that order.
- Please keep your order ID ready. It helps us find your order faster.

## Billing Disputes Team

- For wrong or duplicate charges, fraud and payment disputes, write to billing@shopease.example with your order ID, the date and the last 4 digits of the card.
- The Billing Disputes team replies within 1 business day.

## ShopEase Growth (Marketing) Customers

- Retainer customers can contact their assigned account manager by email, or write to support@shopease.example.

## What Happens After You Contact Us

- You get a ticket number by email within 1 hour.
- Complex cases are passed to a senior support agent, who contacts you within 1 business day.
