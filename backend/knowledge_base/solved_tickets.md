# ShopEase Previously Solved Tickets

Examples of past tickets and the answers that resolved them.

## Solved: Order late by one day

Customer: My order was due yesterday and has not arrived.
Resolution: Explained that orders are considered delayed only after 2 business days past the estimated date. Shared the tracking link from My Orders, Track Package. Customer received the parcel the next day.
Source policy: shipping_policy.md

## Solved: Return of shoes

Customer: I bought shoes last week and they do not fit. I want my money back.
Resolution: Shared the 30-day return window and the 5-step return process. Sent a prepaid return label. Refund was started 2 business days after the warehouse received the shoes and reached the card in 6 days.
Source policy: refund_policy.md

## Solved: Forgotten password

Customer: I forgot my password and cannot log in.
Resolution: Guided the customer to Forgot Password. The email arrived in the spam folder. Reset link worked within its 30-minute validity.
Source policy: account_and_login.md

## Solved: Broken blender

Customer: The blender I received is broken.
Resolution: Customer sent 2 photos within 7 days of delivery. Replacement approved the same day and delivered in 4 business days with free pickup of the broken unit.
Source policy: refund_policy.md and product_faq.md

## Solved: Insights dashboard not updating

Customer: My Insights dashboard shows old data.
Resolution: API key had expired after 90 days. Customer generated a new key and reconnected the data source under Settings, Data Sources.
Source policy: product_faq.md

## Solved: Download link expired

Customer: The e-book download link says expired.
Resolution: Links expire after 72 hours. Customer opened My Library and downloaded again.
Source policy: product_faq.md

## Solved: Temporary card hold

Customer: I see two charges on my card for one order.
Resolution: The second entry was a temporary bank authorisation hold. It disappeared in 4 business days. No refund was needed.
Source policy: billing_and_payments.md
