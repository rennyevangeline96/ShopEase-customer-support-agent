# ShopEase Billing and Payments

## Accepted Payment Methods

- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), UPI, net banking and ShopEase wallet.
- Cash on delivery is available for physical products up to Rs. 10,000.

## Duplicate or Multiple Charges

- If you see the same charge more than once, first check your bank app. Some banks show a temporary authorisation hold that disappears within 3 to 5 business days. A hold is not a real charge.
- If a duplicate charge is real, ShopEase refunds the extra amount automatically within 5 to 7 business days after we confirm it.
- To report it, contact support (email billing@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) with your order ID, the date and the last 4 digits of the card.
- Please do not file a chargeback with your bank before contacting us, because it slows down the refund.

## Disputes and Fraud

- Suspected fraud, unauthorised transactions, chargebacks and payment disputes are handled only by the Billing Disputes team, not by automated support.
- Billing Disputes replies within 1 business day.

## Failed Payments

- If money was deducted but the order was not created, the amount is returned automatically in 5 to 7 business days.
- If the amount is not returned after 7 business days, contact support (email billing@shopease.example or call 1800-123-4567) with your order ID and the last 4 digits of the card.
- For subscriptions, a failed renewal is retried 3 times over 7 days before the plan is paused.

## Invoices

- Invoices are emailed after every payment and are also available in Account Settings, Billing History.
- GST invoices can be generated by adding a GST number under Billing Details before purchase.

## Wallet

- Refunds and coupons are credited to the ShopEase wallet. Wallet balance does not expire for 12 months.
