# ShopEase Company Catalog

ShopEase is an online company with four business lines: physical products, SaaS products, digital products and marketing services. Use this catalog to decide which line a customer question belongs to.

## Physical Products (Category: Product - Physical)

ShopEase Marketplace sells physical goods delivered to the customer's address. Product groups: footwear and apparel (shoes, jackets, sportswear), home appliances (blenders, kettles, air fryers, vacuum cleaners) and consumer electronics (headphones, chargers, smart watches). Typical questions: delivery delays, tracking, damaged or broken items, returns, replacements and refunds.

## ShopEase Storefront (Category: Product - SaaS)

ShopEase Storefront is a SaaS website builder that lets small businesses create and run an online store. It includes drag-and-drop page design, product catalog management, a built-in checkout and a custom domain. It is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription. Typical questions: site not loading, domain connection, checkout settings, plan upgrades and cancellations.

## ShopEase Insights (Category: Product - SaaS)

ShopEase Insights is a SaaS analytics dashboard that shows sales, traffic and customer behaviour for an online store. It connects to Storefront and to other stores through an API key. Typical questions: dashboard not updating, data not syncing, report export errors and API key problems.

## ShopEase Learn (Category: Product - Digital)

ShopEase Learn is a library of digital products: downloadable e-books, video courses and design templates. After purchase the customer gets an instant download link and lifetime access. Typical questions: download link not working, course video not playing, licence key or access problems and refunds for digital items.

## ShopEase Growth (Category: Service - Marketing)

ShopEase Growth is a marketing service run by a human team. It offers SEO optimisation, social media management, email campaigns and paid ads management, sold as monthly retainer packages (Starter, Pro, Premium). Typical questions: campaign status, monthly report timing, changing a package and invoice questions for retainers.

## Account, Login and Billing (Category: Account / Billing)

Applies to every ShopEase product. A single ShopEase account is used to sign in everywhere. Typical questions: forgotten password, locked account, two-factor codes, duplicate or wrong charges, invoices and payment methods.
